# Choosr Visual Direction

## Position

Choosr should feel like a fast, fun plan for tonight, not relationship homework and not a
dating app. The visual system uses a confident midnight canvas, one unmistakable signal
orange, and restrained neutral surfaces. The brand gets its energy from scale, motion,
and shape—not a rainbow of category colors.

## Reference audit

- [Paired](https://www.paired.com/) demonstrates that a couples product can be warm,
  playful, and emotionally safe without becoming childish.
- [Partiful](https://partiful.com/) shows how energetic color, expressive microcopy,
  and visual surprise can make planning feel social rather than administrative.
- [Tinder](https://tinder.com/download/) established that a visible card stack and
  immediate binary actions make swiping understandable without instruction.
- [Letterboxd](https://letterboxd.com/) demonstrates how a dark foundation allows
  varied entertainment artwork and category colors to remain coherent.

These are category references, not templates. Choosr's own signature is a **bold C whose
opening resolves into a decisive check**.

## System

- Background: midnight navy `#07111F`
- Primary brand/action: signal orange `#FF5A1F`
- Surfaces: slate navy `#0F1B2D` and `#172842`
- Supporting content blue: `#2563EB`
- Match/success only: green `#22C55E`
- Typography: oversized, dense headings; compact uppercase status labels; readable body
  copy with generous line height
- Shape language: rounded cards, soft pills, circular actions, small rotated match badges
- Motion: short spring responses and restrained scale/opacity changes; never obscure the
  current decision

## App icon

The icon is a single oversized ink-navy C with an integrated check-shaped opening on a
signal-orange field. It contains no dating symbolism, remains recognizable at launcher
size, and retains safe padding for iOS masks and Android adaptive-icon crops.

Source assets live in `assets/brand/`. Generated platform sizes live in the native iOS
and Android asset catalogs.
